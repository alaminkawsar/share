### Important: understand the legal arrangement

Bangladesh Bank's import rules and Bangladesh Customs documentation distinguish between the importer and the C&F agent.

* The importer needs a valid IRC unless exempted.

* An LC is not the only permitted payment method. Eligible imports can be made against a purchase/sales contract without an LC, subject to bank and import-policy requirements.

* Customs documentation must properly identify the importer and shipment. Bangladesh Bank +3

My recommendation: Ask the company to import and resell the toys to you locally under its own commercial import arrangement. That is clearer than simply paying a cargo agent to ship goods under somebody else's paperwork.

Before paying, ask for:

* Its valid commercial IRC.

* A written quotation including product, freight, customs duty, VAT and service fees.

* Confirmation of who will be named as importer on the Bill of Entry.

* A proper local invoice for your purchase.

* Confirmation that the toys are permitted for import and their correct HS code.

You can verify import requirements through [Bangladesh Customs Import Export Hub](https://hub.bangladeshcustoms.gov.bd/?utm_source=chatgpt.com).
